Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

This Policy explains what data the Rytm app ("the app", "we") collects, how we use and store it, and what rights you have as a user. Rytm is a personal tracker for work shifts, finances, and debt/settlement schedules; it is not a legal entity or a commercial company — it is developed and maintained by a single individual.

What data we collect

Account data. To sign in to the app, you register with email and password, or with a Google account. In the first case we store your email; in the second, the name, email, and profile photo that Google provides at sign-in.

Data you enter yourself. Everything you enter in the app is stored exclusively for you:

  • Financial transactions: amounts, categories, subcategories, tags, wallets, comments, dates;
  • Wallets, exchange rates, budgets, and recurring transactions you set up;
  • Your work shift schedule and shift types;
  • Settlement records (debts, installment plans) and their payment history.

Data kept only on your device. Your access PIN is stored in hashed form (SHA-256) locally in the browser and is never transmitted to any server — not even we can see it. The same applies to your theme choice (dark/light).

How we use your data

Data is used solely to run the app itself: showing your statistics, calculating balances, and syncing between your devices. We do not show ads, do not use third-party analytics or trackers, and do not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing or any other purposes.

Where data is stored

Data is stored in the Firebase cloud database (a Google service), which acts as the technical infrastructure provider. Each account has access only to its own data — this is enforced by server-side access rules (Firestore Security Rules), and no other app user can see or change your records.

Your rights

  • View, edit, or delete any of your own records directly in the app at any time;
  • Export your financial transactions as CSV (the "Export CSV" button on the Finance tab);
  • Permanently delete your account and all associated data — the account-deletion button in the app does this immediately and irreversibly (see the account deletion page for details);
  • Contact us with any questions about your data at the contact below.

Security

The connection to the server is always encrypted (HTTPS). Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. You can additionally enable a local PIN code to quickly lock the app on your device.

Children

The app is not intended for individuals under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The last-updated date is always shown at the top of this page.

Contact
