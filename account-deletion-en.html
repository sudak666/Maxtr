Account and data deletion

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Rytm lets you delete your account and all data associated with it at any time — directly in the app, with no need to write to us or wait for a reply. This page describes how to do that, and exactly what gets deleted.

How to delete your account

1
Open Rytm (in a browser at maxtr-c238f.web.app or in the Android app) and sign in to your account.
2
Go to Settings → Account.
3
Tap "Delete account" and confirm in the dialog that appears.
Open Rytm →

Can't sign in to the app?

If you've lost access to your account (for example, to the Google account linked to your sign-in) and can't use the delete button yourself, write to maxim19800606@gmail.com from the same email address linked to the account — we'll delete it manually within a few days.

What exactly gets deleted

Deletion is irreversible and happens immediately after confirmation:

  • The account itself (Google or email+password sign-in) — signing in with it again will no longer be possible;
  • All financial transactions, wallets, exchange rates, budgets, recurring transactions, tags;
  • Your work shift schedule and shift types;
  • Settlement records (debts, installment plans) and payment history;
  • The local PIN code and biometric settings on this device.

Data that never leaves your device in the first place (such as the local cache) is also cleared the next time you open the app after account deletion.

Questions

Details on exactly what data Rytm collects and how it's processed are in the Privacy Policy. For any other questions, write to maxim19800606@gmail.com.
