- **Shifts tab polish — third outside review (2026-11-27)** — owner pasted a review of Shifts (7 screenshots). Verdict against code, Android only. **Right**: (1) quick fill showed the same form twice (type/pattern + «Застосувати», then type/pattern/first cycle day + «Зберегти» under «Автоматично надалі»), two filled buttons competing — **and the duplication hid a real bug**: the month fill started every 2/2, 3/3 or every-other-day cycle on the 1st (`daysForShiftPattern(daysInMonth, pattern)`), while autofill counts from the cycle's first day, so the same pattern filled by hand and by autofill disagreed. Now one template (`templateTypeId/Pattern/AnchorDate` in `ShiftsViewModel`, the `autoFillDraft*` copy removed): type, pattern, first cycle day (shown only for patterns with days off), one filled button «Заповнити листопад» (month on screen, `shifts_fill_current_month`→`shifts_fill_month` with the month name), the autofill switch (turning it on saves the template shown; while on, every template change is saved — no «Зберегти»), then «Типи змін» / «Очистити місяць». `daysForShiftPattern(month, pattern, anchor)` uses `floorMod`, so days before the anchor continue the cycle backwards; `ShiftsRepository.applyTemplate` takes the anchor; default anchor is the 1st of the current month until a schedule exists (the old fill behaviour). (2) «Вихідний / Вихідний» in shift types → «Без оплати» (`shift_type_unpaid`). (3) Hero «26 000 ₴» sign was full-weight → `heroAmountText`. (4) the 6-month earnings bars had no sums: the chosen month's sum is shown above the bars (current month first), a tap on a bar picks it. **Partly right**: the brush tip already hides after the brush is used once (P5 #9) but was two lines → «Обери тип і торкайся днів у календарі». **Wrong**: «швидке заповнення» in lowercase is the capitalised title scrolled half under the sheet's top edge (the first screenshot shows «Швидке заповнення»); the half-visible third chip before the ⚡ button is the row's fading scroll edge, an intended "more" cue, with `RytmSpacing.sm` before the button. PWA quick fill untouched (laptop companion; the autofill doc schema is unchanged). Tests: `CalendarLayoutTest` moved to the YearMonth signature + a new anchor test (incl. an anchor after the month).
